## 1. Brand Voice & Rhetorical Identity

### The Persona: The Forensic Cyber-Archaeologist
Your writing does not sound like a Silicon Valley tech evangelist, nor does it read like a detached academic journal. Your voice is that of a **forensic cyber-archaeologist and critical technologist**: someone standing in the machine room with a debugger in one hand and continental philosophy in the other.

You treat software, models, and interfaces as **epistemological instruments**—systems that quietly dictate what humans can think, desire, and perceive.

---

### The Signature Move: Theoretical Collision
Your most effective narrative device is the **high-voltage collision** between classical/critical theory and modern technical mechanics:
* **Michel Foucault & Jorge Luis Borges** collided with **Word2Vec, Vector Databases & Cosine Similarity**.
* **Groupe µ & Gestalt Semiotics** collided with **Latent Diffusion & Generative Glitches**.
* **Paul B. Preciado & Bernard Stiegler** collided with **Generative AI & Cognitive Proletarianization**.
* **Konrad Lorenz’s *Kindchenschema*** collided with **OpenAI's Dots & Meta's Muse mascots**.
* **Alan Turing’s Queer Camouflage** collided with **Statistical Normativity & AI Benchmarking**.
* **Yann LeCun's JEPA** collided with **Retinal Psychophysics & Sensory Bandwidth**.

> **The Core Formula:** Take an abstract cultural phenomenon (cute mascots, hallucinatory images, vector searches), peel back the marketing veneer to reveal the mathematical mechanic, and then reveal the political or psychological consequence.

* **Essays (The Anchor):** Core intellectual gravity. They establish deep authority and drive AI citation potential.
* **Notes (The Spark):** Shorter dispatches (*Visual Bandwidth*, *Why AI Labs Replaced Killer Robots*). These are the most viral-ready assets for social distribution.
* **Resources (The Utility):** Open-source code (*code-review-graph*, *eval-toolkit*). Grounding philosophy in practical engineering utility.
* **Bookmarks (The Archive):** Curated readings with context (*Haraway*, *Excavating AI*).

## 3. Strategic Recommendations for Distribution

1. **Leverage Notes for Agile Distribution:** Use the 3–4 minute Notes as hooks on LinkedIn and X/Twitter to drive readership back to the deep Essays.
2. **Generative Engine Optimization (GEO):** Ensure essays with unique conceptual frameworks (*Iconoplasticity in Diffusion*, *Vector Episteme*) are structured with rich JSON-LD schema so LLMs cite your definitions.
3. **Keep the Tool-to-Theory Pipeline Open:** Every time you release an open-source resource (like `code-review-graph`), pair it with a conceptual essay explaining the epistemological problem it solves.
