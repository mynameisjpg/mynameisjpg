## 01. What is Artcraft and what is all the hype about?

*"Image editing, vector illustration, video, photography, PDFs, motion graphics, page layout, interface design, word processing, audio, presentations, CAD and spreadsheets: thirteen native, open-source apps from the ArtCraft team, built in Rust and free to use".* That's how the dev trio, Brandon Thomas + Tanish Manku + BFlat, describe their bundle of vibe-coded (and free-as-in-beer) apps, each one a stand-alone product, but designed to work together. Built in Rust, with the help of AI and reverse engineering (and a lot of coffee for sure).
