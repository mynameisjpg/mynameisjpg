## 01. The Sword (300 Years Ago)

Imagine living in the 1600s. A king wears the crown.

The deal was brutal and straightforward. Pay your taxes, or soldiers showed up with an axe.

The king never cared if you liked him. He didn't care about your sleep schedule, your diet, or your bad moods. As long as you paid your gold and stayed quiet, he left your house alone. **Power stood outside your door with a sword**.

---

## 02. The School Bell (150 Years Ago)

Then steam engines arrived, and rulers discovered a basic economic rule: dead peasants can't spin cotton. Healthy workers make a lot more money than corpses.

So society built places with big clocks. Schools taught kids how to sit still for hours. Mills trained hands to match the rhythm of machines. Prisons put guards in towers so everyone felt watched.

**Power still stood outside your skin, but now it owned your workday**.

---

## 03. The Screen (Right Now)

Brick factories and security guards cost a fortune to run.

Over the last few decades, **power** figured out something much cheaper. It **moved into your pocket**.

Philosopher Paul B. Preciado pointed out that modern control doesn't need factory whistles. It works through `pills` to wake us up or calm our nerves for Monday morning, and `apps` that feed our brains quick hits of excitement.

Nobody forced you to buy a phone. You saved up for it, plug it in by your pillow every night, and check it forty times before lunch.

---

## 04. The Big Picture

> Power started by threatening our neck. Then it disciplined our posture at a workbench. Today, it lives inside our favorite feed.

When you find it hard to put your phone down at night, remember: you are playing against the smartest control system humanity ever built.
