Turing & Queer AI: Synthetic Bodies, Mimicry, and Representation

BY JUAN PABLO GIUSEPPONI

Alan Turing’s 1950 'Imitation Game' was born from non-normative identity. By investigating how algorithmic systems mimic human behavior, we uncover how synthetic media redefines representation, performance, and bodily subversion.

"The institution of statistical identity is not a neutral mirror; it is an active filter through which non-normative agency is continuously negotiated."

When generative neural networks sample latent space distributions, they construct statistical norms. This essay explores how queer theory provides a framework for resistance against algorithmic categorization.
