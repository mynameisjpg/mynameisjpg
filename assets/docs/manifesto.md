# [ OPEN NOTEBOOK // WHY THIS EXISTS ]

I run this space out of pure curiosity.

Whenever I read across design, tech, culture, AI, psychology and philosophy, I start seeing weird patterns emerge. When those dots connect with what I've read, I'll write about them, sometimes as a deep essay, sometimes as a quick field note, and other times just by saving great tools, papers, and links that I find interesting.

A quick disclaimer on how I write: my posts can sound dead certain, but that's just how my brain tests theory. I push arguments as far as they take me, to see what holds up and what breaks. What may read as verbosity is most often my strict adherence to definitions, or thinking with a systemic approach. In reality, I don't have anything figured out —I'm just exploring out loud and trying to make sense of a fast-moving world.

This is an open workbench. No preaching, no sycophancy and no pretense. Just solid resources, honest observations, and patterns worth thinking through.

Take what’s useful, challenge what isn’t, and feel free to think alongside me.
